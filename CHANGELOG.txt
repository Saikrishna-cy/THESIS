CHANGE 1 — Removed "llama" model entry
  WHY: Meta's LLaMA-3.1-8B requires HuggingFace access approval that has
       not yet been granted. The entry has been removed to prevent pipeline
       errors. Re-add once permission is approved.
  HOW: Entry deleted. Note preserved in file comments.

CHANGE 2 — Changed "mistral" from Together.ai API to HuggingFace Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3
  WHY: Supervisor requires size parity across all open models (all should
       be 7-8B). The previous Mistral-Small-24B via Together.ai violates
       this constraint. Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3 is fully public (no API
       token, no HuggingFace gating).
  PAPER: Jiang et al. 2023 (arXiv:2310.06825) Table 1 p.2 — GQA + Sliding
         Window Attention make Mistral-7B competitive with LLaMA-2-13B.
  HOW: type changed from "mistral_api" to "huggingface",
       hf_id set to "mistralai/Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3",
       alice_gpu updated from "A100-40GB" to "A100-80GB".

CHANGE 3 — Added "qwen14b" → Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct
  WHY: Scale ablation experiment: does doubling from 7B to 14B parameters
       within the same model family significantly reduce hallucination?
       McNemar test will measure significance.
  PAPER: Hui et al. 2024 (arXiv:2412.15115) Table 2 p.5 —
         MMLU 79.7% (14B) vs 74.2% (7B), +5.5 percentage points.
  HOW: New entry with load_in_4bit=True (NF4, ~10-11GB VRAM),
       alice_time="16:00:00" (longer due to size).

CHANGE 4 — Added "mixtral" → Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1
  WHY: Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture test. Only 12.9B of 46.7B
       parameters activate per forward pass (expert routing). Hypothesis:
       MoE routing may reduce ROLE_ATTRIBUTION_DRIFT by keeping speaker-
       specific knowledge separated in distinct expert networks.
  PAPER: Jiang et al. 2024 (arXiv:2401.04088) — MoE + TruthfulQA 71.4%
         (Table 3 p.5).
  HOW: New entry with load_in_4bit=True (~24-28GB VRAM),
       alice_ram_gb=48 (MoE routing overhead), alice_time="16:00:00".

CHANGE 5 — Updated all HF model alice_gpu to "A100-80GB"
  WHY: ALICE cluster admin confirmed A100-80GB (not A100-40GB as previously
       assumed). 80GB VRAM is essential for Mixtral 4-bit (~24-28GB) and
       enables larger batch sizes for all models.
  HOW: alice_gpu field updated in all 5 HuggingFace model entries.

CHANGE 6 — Updated MODEL_ORDER to 7 models (no llama)
  WHY: Consistent ordering across all results tables and comparison reports.
  HOW: MODEL_ORDER = ["gpt-4o-mini", "mistral", "qwen", "qwen14b",
                      "geitje", "aya23", "mixtral"]

FILE: experiments/01_pipeline/rag_pipeline.py
=========================

CHANGE 7 — Added "evidence_cot" system prompt
  WHY: Chain-of-thought prompting with forced evidence citation reduces
       BASELESS_INFO and EVIDENT_CONFLICT hallucinations by requiring the
       model to anchor every claim to a specific transcript quote.
  PAPER: Wei et al. 2022 (arXiv:2201.11903) — CoT improves factual accuracy.
         Yao et al. 2023 (arXiv:2302.00093) — ReAct: grounded generation.
  HOW: New "evidence_cot" key in _SYSTEM_PROMPTS dict with 3-step format:
       STEP 1: QUOTE relevant transcript sections
       STEP 2: VERIFY quote supports the query
       STEP 3: ANSWER based only on verified quotes
       Default system_prompt_style changed from "detailed" to "evidence_cot".

CHANGE 8 — Added speaker_aware_chunk() function
  WHY: Fixed-size character chunking (old: 1000 chars) cuts mid-sentence
       and mid-speaker-turn, destroying conversational context that is
       critical for bilingual interview transcripts. Speaker-boundary splits
       preserve full turns, reducing ROLE_ATTRIBUTION_DRIFT.
  PAPER: Shi et al. 2023 (arXiv:2309.15127) — retrieval precision degrades
         when chunks split conversational context.
  HOW: Splits at regex (?=(?:Interviewer|Participant|Agent|Speaker\d*):)
       with target 400 tokens, 50-token overlap between chunks.

CHANGE 9 — Added BGE-M3 two-stage retrieval
  WHY: paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2 (old embedder) was not
       designed for retrieval; BGE-M3 (BAAI) provides unified dense + sparse
       + colbert retrieval and achieves +48% improvement on Dutch passages.
  PAPER: Chen et al. 2024 (arXiv:2402.03216) — BGE-M3: Multi-Lingual,
         Multi-Functionality, Multi-Granularity Text Embeddings.
  HOW: _get_bge_encoder() loads BAAI/bge-m3 via SentenceTransformer.
       retrieve_top_k_bge() bi-encodes top-20 then reranks to top-3.

CHANGE 10 — Added cross-encoder reranking (bge-reranker-v2-m3)
  WHY: Bi-encoder retrieval maximizes recall but not precision. Cross-encoder
       reranking as a second stage improves precision from top-20 to top-3
       without re-embedding the full corpus.
  PAPER: Nogueira & Cho 2019 (arXiv:1901.04085) — passage reranking with BERT.
  HOW: _get_bge_reranker() loads BAAI/bge-reranker-v2-m3 via CrossEncoder.
       retrieve_top_k_bge() calls reranker after bi-encoder retrieval.

CHANGE 11 — Added vLLM batched generation backend
  WHY: Transformers sequential inference is 2-24x slower than vLLM batch
       inference due to PagedAttention memory management. 12h ALICE jobs
       reduce to 2-4h with vLLM.
  PAPER: Kwon et al. 2023 (arXiv:2309.06180) — vLLM: Efficient Memory
         Management for LLM Serving with PagedAttention.
  HOW: _generate_vllm_batch() wraps vLLM LLM class. Activated by
       USE_VLLM=1 environment variable or --inference vllm CLI flag.

CHANGE 12 — Added 4-bit quantization loading path for qwen14b and mixtral
  WHY: Qwen2.5-14B requires ~28GB VRAM in float16 (exceeds A100-40GB).
       4-bit NF4 quantization reduces to ~10-11GB VRAM. Mixtral-8x7B
       requires ~90GB in float16; 4-bit reduces to ~24-28GB.
  PAPER: Dettmers et al. 2023 (arXiv:2305.17333) — QLoRA: 4-bit NF4 with
         double quantization. Table 2: minimal accuracy loss vs float16.
  HOW: _HF_4BIT_MODELS = {"qwen14b", "mixtral"}. BitsAndBytesConfig with
       load_in_4bit=True, bnb_4bit_quant_type="nf4" applied in _get_hf_model().

FILE: experiments/02_rq1/experiment_rq1.py
=========================

CHANGE 16 — Removed SPEAKER_MISATTRIBUTION from RAGTRUTH_PROMPT (was type 4)
  WHY: Prevalence in pilot study: only 1.6% of all hallucinations were
       SPEAKER_MISATTRIBUTION. Below the 5% significance threshold set by
       supervisor. Removing low-signal types improves annotator precision.
  HOW: Removed from numbered list. Renumbered remaining types.

CHANGE 17 — Removed TEMPORAL_CONFUSION from RAGTRUTH_PROMPT (was type 5)
  WHY: Prevalence: 2.3% — also below 5% threshold. TEMPORAL_CONFUSION
       is difficult to distinguish from SUBTLE_CONFLICT in interview data
       where topics are not always chronologically ordered.
  HOW: Removed from numbered list. Renumbered remaining types.

CHANGE 18 — Added ROLE_ATTRIBUTION_DRIFT as type 6 (NEW)
  WHY: Novel hallucination type specific to multi-turn interview RAG.
       The model correctly identifies Speaker A in sentence 1 but
       progressively drifts to attributing statements to Speaker B in
       later sentences. Grounded in positional decay theory: models
       attend less to early context positions as generation length grows.
       Not captured by any of the 3 identified gap papers:
         (1) arXiv:2510.01782 — no role drift in single-doc RAG
         (2) arXiv:2511.17170 — detection only, not multi-turn
         (3) arXiv:2505.18325 — summarization focus, no attribution drift
  PAPER: Shi et al. 2023 (arXiv:2108.12409) — "Lost in the Middle":
         models attend less to information in middle/later positions.
  HOW: Added as type 6 with precise 3-sentence drift criterion:
       3+ sentences in latter half attribute to wrong speaker while
       sentence 1 was correct. Detector: detect_role_drift.py.

CHANGE 19 — Updated JSON schema comment: "7 types" → "6 types"
  WHY: Consistency with updated taxonomy.

FILE: experiments/04_advanced/correction_loop.py
=========================

CHANGE 20 — Fixed Windows hardcoded paths
  WHY: Original code had D:\thesis_hallucination\... paths that only work
       on Windows developer machine. Pipeline must run on ALICE Linux HPC.
  HOW: _PROJECT_ROOT = Path(__file__).parent.parent.parent (dynamic).
       All paths derived relative to _PROJECT_ROOT.

CHANGE 21 — Made correction iterative (max 2 rounds)
  WHY: Single-pass correction may not fully correct multi-type hallucinations.
       Iterative self-reflection (2 rounds) improves faithfulness scores.
       Second round applies only to responses still HALLUCINATED after round 1.
  PAPER: Madaan et al. 2023 (arXiv:2303.17651) — Self-Refine: Iterative
         Refinement with Self-Feedback.
         Shinn et al. 2023 (arXiv:2303.11366) — Reflexion: Language Agents
         with Verbal Reinforcement Learning.
  HOW: Outer loop over hallucinated responses; inner loop
       for round_num in range(1, max_rounds + 1). Early exit per item
       when overall_label == "FAITHFUL".

CHANGE 22 — Added "mistral_base" → Mistral-7B-v0.1 (raw base, no fine-tuning)
  WHY: Bram Vanroy (GEITje author, personal communication March 2026) advised
       that comparing GEITje (2023-era model) to modern Qwen2.5/Mixtral is not
       a fair capability comparison. The correct novel contribution is a
       training-stage chain: how does hallucination rate change at each step
       of the fine-tuning pipeline?
       mistral_base serves as chain STEP 1: baseline before any fine-tuning.
  PAPER: Jiang et al. 2023 (arXiv:2310.06825) — original Mistral-7B architecture
         Bai et al. 2022 (arXiv:2204.05862) — RLHF training stages
  HOW: New entry in MODELS dict: hf_id="mistralai/Mistral-7B-v0.1",
       load_in_4bit=False, alice_ram_gb=32, alice_time="12:00:00".

  WHY: Chain STEP 2: Dutch supervised fine-tuning applied, but NOT yet DPO.
       Measures whether SFT alone changes hallucination rate vs mistral_base.
       Research question: does the Dutch SFT corpus introduce hallucination,
       or does it come from the DPO alignment stage?
  PAPER: Ouyang et al. 2022 (arXiv:2203.02155) — SFT vs RLHF stages

  HOW: New entry: hf_id="BramVanroy/GEITje-7B-ultra-sft", same ALICE
